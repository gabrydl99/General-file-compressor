General File Compressor for Android — compress and convert videos, photos, PDFs, audio and Office files on your phone, fully offline (same FFmpeg engine as the Windows app).

**Install:** download `General-File-Compressor.apk` on the phone and open it. Allow installing from this source when Android asks; if Play Protect warns, choose **Install anyway** (the app isn't on the Play Store).

**First start:** tap **Allow** on the "Allow access to all files" card, so the app can move originals into the Original folder and save in any folder.

- Percentage, Size in MB or Convert only; presets for WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram and Email
- Video: MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, or audio only as MP3/M4A — two-pass H.264 for accurate sizes
- Photos: JPG, PNG, WebP (also reads iPhone HEIC); Audio: MP3, M4A, OGG, Opus
- PDF and Word/PowerPoint/Excel: the pictures inside are re-compressed
- Results in Download/General File Compressor/Compressed, originals moved to …/Original
- Share → General File Compressor from the Gallery, WhatsApp or Files; keeps working with the screen off
